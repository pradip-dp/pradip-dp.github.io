About

I'm a Formal Verification Engineer with over two years of experience specializing in building formal environments for cutting-edge IP blocks. My expertise lies in formal property verification, with a track record of identifying 60+ critical corner case bugs in production designs.

Currently at Synopsys Inc's Formal Verification Centre of Excellence, I previously led a team of five engineers at Intel, driving verification sign-offs for datacenter products. I'm pioneering the integration of GenAI methodologies into formal verification, reducing verification cycles and enhancing test coverage by up to 80%.

Best Paper Award · DVCon India 2024

GenAI Leap in Formal Verification Testplanning

Authors: Anshul Jain, Karan Rawat, Pradip Prajapati

First-of-its-kind research integrating Generative AI into formal verification testplanning. Developed a novel framework using chain-of-thought prompt engineering to build exhaustive formal verification testplans, achieving 80-90% bug coverage across different design classes.

Impact: 80% reduction in verification ramp-up time

Engineering IP Track · DAC 2024

Watt's Up with DDR5: Formal Verification Framework for Robust DRAM Power Management

Published comprehensive DRAM power management verification framework at Design Automation Conference, San Francisco. Framework ensures shift-left verification and power consumption reduction through exhaustive formal proofs.

Achievement: Identified 45+ bugs and power enhancement opportunities in cutting-edge server designs

Conference Presentation · DAC 2026

No Credit Where Credit Is Due: Quiesced Formal Check for PCIe Crediting

Authors: Isha Lale, Pradip Prajapati, Anshul Jain

Presented a formal-property-based methodology for exhaustive validation of PCIe flow control crediting. The work introduces a quiesced check to prove forward progress when receiver buffer space is available and demonstrates how the approach uncovered a critical shared-credit handling bug.

Highlight: Exposed a real mode-switching credit bug that standard checker flows can miss

Research Paper · DVCon India 2025 & VLSID 2026

RAG for Formal-Aware Property Generation

Extended research on applying Retrieval-Augmented Generation techniques to formal verification. Building on GenAI testplanning work to create formal-aware property generation systems leveraging domain-specific knowledge bases.
